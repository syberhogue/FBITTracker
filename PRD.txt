PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)
OntarioTech FBIT Tracker (v2.0 Architecture & Feature Rollout)
Faculty of Business and Information Technology — Ontario Tech UniversityEXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PRODUCT VISION1.1 OverviewThe OntarioTech FBIT Tracker is a centralized administrative and academic operationsplatform engineered specifically for the Faculty of Business and InformationTechnology (FBIT) at Ontario Tech University. It streamlines critical academicand operational workflows—such as end-of-term grade submission tracking, historicalgrade benchmarking, graduate thesis defense milestones, and academic researchpaper submissions—into an intuitive, role-tailored dashboard.1.2 ObjectivesUpgrade prototype architectures to a production-ready Web Application.Provide real-time milestone tracking across administrative, academic, and research processes.Enable CSV-driven course grade distribution uploads and historical benchmarking.Support multi-term comparative analysis for faculty quality assurance and Dean review.Deliver automated checklist status updates for faculty grade submissions.TECHNICAL STACK & SYSTEM ARCHITECTURES2.1 Frontend StackFramework: React 18+ with ViteStyling: Tailwind CSS & Lucide React IconographyData Visualization: Recharts (Line Charts, Bar Charts, Comparative Histograms)Architecture: Component-driven Single-Page Application (SPA)2.2 Backend & Infrastructure StackBackend Engine: Local Supabase Docker ContainerDatabase: PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security (RLS)Authentication: Supabase GoTrue (Email/Password & Role-Based Access)Storage: Supabase Storage Buckets for CSV/Syllabus Document StorageRealtime Engine: Supabase Realtime for live milestone updatesUSER ROLES & PERMISSIONS3.1 Dean & Academic DirectorsFull read/write access across all departmental trackers.Ability to view faculty-wide grade submission progress and flag distribution anomalies.Authority to approve or override stage completion statuses.3.2 Department Chairs & Program LeadsManagement access for specific program areas (IT, Business Administration).Ability to generate grade submission export reports and outstanding checklist summaries.3.3 Faculty Instructors & LecturersAbility to view assigned course trackers and milestone deadlines.Capability to upload grade CSV files for automated processing and distribution calculation.Manual toggle override for submission checklists.3.4 Research Leads & Graduate SupervisorsAccess to dedicated Thesis Defense Trackers and Conference Paper Submission timelines.Milestone customization and supervisor review capabilities.CORE FEATURE MODULES & FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS4.1 Dashboard Overview & AnalyticsMetrics Cards: Displaying Active Trackers, Overdue Stages, Upcoming Deadlines (14-day window), and Completed Milestones.Central Process Table: Comprehensive view showing tracker type, category, mandatory progress percentages, and status badges (Completed, In Progress, Attention Required).4.2 Process Milestone Timeline VisualizerInteractive Node Timeline: Visual step-by-step progress tracking with dynamic status color coding (Green = Completed, Red = Overdue, Blue = Active, Gray = Skipped).Stage Checklist: Detailed listing with descriptions, target due dates, mandatory vs. optional flags, and status controls.4.3 Course Grade Analytics & BenchmarkingSingle Course vs History Mode: Evaluates class average, pass rate, and student enrollment trends over multiple academic terms (F24 through F26).Grade Distribution Histograms: Visual breakdown of letter grades (A, B, C, D, F).CSV Grade Upload Pipeline: Automated parsing of standard CSV grade sheets (StudentID, Name, FinalGrade) with instant recalculation of metrics.Multi-Course Comparative Benchmarking: Side-by-side term comparison across selected courses to evaluate academic consistency and flag courses requiring intervention.4.4 Submissions Status ChecklistAutomated Tracking: Cross-references course grade uploads to mark submissions as completed.Target Course Management: Upload custom target course lists via CSV.Status Filtering & Search: Filter by All, Submitted, or Outstanding courses.Export Capabilities: Generate downloadable CSV reports of outstanding faculty submissions for administrative follow-up.DATA MODEL SCHEMA (POSTGRESQL)5.1 Table: profilesid (UUID, PK, FK to auth.users)full_name (TEXT)email (TEXT)role (TEXT: 'dean', 'chair', 'instructor', 'student')department (TEXT)created_at (TIMESTAMPTZ)5.2 Table: trackersid (UUID, PK)title (TEXT)type (TEXT: 'general', 'grade-submission')category (TEXT: 'Administrative', 'Academic', 'Research')description (TEXT)created_by (UUID, FK to profiles.id)created_at (TIMESTAMPTZ)5.3 Table: stagesid (UUID, PK)tracker_id (UUID, FK to trackers.id)title (TEXT)description (TEXT)due_date (DATE)is_required (BOOLEAN)status (TEXT: 'pending', 'in-progress', 'completed', 'skipped')5.4 Table: course_termsid (UUID, PK)course_code (TEXT)course_title (TEXT)department (TEXT)instructor (TEXT)term_code (TEXT: 'F24', 'W25', 'S25', 'F25', 'W26', 'F26')class_average (NUMERIC)pass_rate (NUMERIC)enrolled_count (INT)distribution_json (JSONB)SECURITY & COMPLIANCERow-Level Security (RLS) enabled on all tables.Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) ensuring instructors only edit authorized course terms.Secure, client-side CSV processing to maintain FERPA/FIPA compliance prior to server upload.FUTURE EXTENSIBILITY & ROADMAPAutomated email notification triggers via SMTP/SendGrid integration for approaching deadlines.Banner/MyCampus SIS API integration for direct grade synchronization.AI-driven grade anomaly detection to flag historical variance
